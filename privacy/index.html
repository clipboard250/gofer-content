Privacy

Your info stays yours

Last updated: October 27, 2026

The short version

  • This site sets no cookies.
  • Visits are counted as totals, with no cookies and nothing that identifies you.
  • No ads, and nothing sold or shared.
  • If you send a message, Gofer Content uses it only to reply.

Who we are

Gofer Content is a website studio in St. Petersburg, Florida, run by Dayna, Director of Online Presence. Questions about this page go to info@gofercontent.com.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and runs no advertising or tracking tools that follow you around the web. That is why you never see a cookie pop-up here.

To know how many people visit, the site uses a privacy-friendly visitor counter. It counts page visits, which site sent you, your device type, and your country, all as totals. It uses no cookies, never identifies you, and never follows you to other sites.

Prefer not to be counted at all? Tap the button below. This browser will be left out from then on.

One small note stays in your own browser: when you close the free website check pop-up, your browser remembers it so the pop-up doesn't show again. That note never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

What you send us

The site only receives what you type into a form:

  • Contact form: your name, email, phone, website, and message.
  • Free website check: your website address and phone number.

Forms are delivered by Formspree, a form service that emails your message to Gofer Content and keeps a copy in our Formspree account. The website check also sends your website address to Gofer Content's own scoring tool, hosted on Vercel, to calculate your score.

Gofer Content uses this information only to reply to you and to help with your website. It is never sold, rented, or used for ads.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may briefly record technical details such as your IP address to deliver the page and keep it secure.

Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, Gofer Content honors it. Since nothing here is sold or shared, there is nothing to switch off.

Your choices

Wherever you live, you can ask to see, correct, or delete anything you sent us. Email info@gofercontent.com and you'll hear back within 30 days. Visitors in the EU or UK may also contact their local data protection authority.

How long we keep it

Messages are kept only as long as needed to work with you. Ask anytime and yours will be deleted.

Kids

This site is not meant for children under 13, and Gofer Content does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If anything here changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change with it.
