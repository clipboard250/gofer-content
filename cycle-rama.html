Where It All Began
Our Story

Built on Passion.
Proven by Performance.

Founded by Wes Brown, Cycle-Rama has been at the forefront of motorcycle performance innovation for nearly five decades. What started as a commitment to making Harleys faster has evolved into a legacy that riders across the country depend on.

We specialize in performance parts tailored for M8 and Twin Cam engines, with a focus on camshafts that deliver proven performance and durability. Every part is designed, tested, and proven on our in-house dyno.
