Our Story

Built on Passion.
Proven on the Dyno.

Wes Brown started Cycle-Rama in 1976 with one idea: make Harleys faster and make them last. Fifty years and ten Brute Horsepower Shootout wins later, that idea hasn't changed — just gotten sharper.

We design proprietary cam profiles with ramps that open slow, ramp hard, and close clean — building port velocity and cylinder fill that factory engineers leave on the table. Every grind is tested on our in-house dyno. Every set of CNC heads is validated on our flow bench. No guesswork. No marketing fiction.

Numbers Don't Lie.

Every Cycle-Rama cam profile is validated on our in-house dyno. Not a simulator. Not a borrowed chart from someone else's build. Real HP and torque from real engines we built in this shop.

Our flow bench uses custom valve-opening fixtures designed and manufactured by our in-house machinist to measure flow rates at various opening heights. You get data, not promises.
