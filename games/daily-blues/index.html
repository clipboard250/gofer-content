How to play

Every day brings five facts from blues history tied to that date, each from a different decade.

Swipe right if it's true, left if it's false, or tap the buttons. On every false card, one detail is wrong: a player, a label, a city, an instrument.

Each answer comes with the real story and its sources. New cards at midnight. Play past days in the Archive.
