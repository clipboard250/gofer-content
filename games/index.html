    Questions

    Can I put a game or quiz on my own website?

    Yes. Gofer Content builds the game, quiz, poll, or survey right into your website, in your colors and your voice. You own it, and fans play it on your site, not someone else's app.

    Do fans need to sign up or download anything to play?

    No. Fans tap and play on any phone or computer. Scores and streaks stay on the player's own phone, so there is no account and no app to download.

    Will a game slow down my website?

    No. Gofer Content builds every game by hand with no heavy add-ons. This page, with playable samples on it, is built to the same speed standard as the rest of gofercontent.com.

    How does a game help people find my business?

    A game gives people a reason to come back and a reason to share. Every shared score is a link back to your website, and each game page is set up so Google and AI answers can read what it is about.

    Where do survey answers go?

    Straight to your email inbox. Every form includes a spam trap, and Gofer Content gets a copy so nothing gets lost.
