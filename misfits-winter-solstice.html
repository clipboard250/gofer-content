What it includes

A winter scene in the desert. A big sled, a few small spaces, a dock, and a fire. Play, reflection, and community.

🛷 The sled

Climb, slide, laugh. Simple.

🏠 The shanties

Small rooms with different moods inside each one.

🎣 The dock

A place to sit, listen, and stare at “ice” that complains.

🔥 The fire

Warmth, stories, and the kind of talk you do not schedule.

🚗 The car

Bench seats, radio, secret transmissions, tiny time capsule.

Built with reuse in mind after debut.
