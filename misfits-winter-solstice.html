What this is

A walk-in winter scene built around three things: play, reflection, and community. A giant runner sled on a “snow” mound, shanties on a “frozen” lake, and spaces to gather—campfire, dock, and a vintage car.

🛷 The sled

Climb. slide. laugh. There’s a fort hiding in there for the kid part of your brain.

🏠 The shanties

Small rooms with big moods—games, writing, sound, quiet, surprise. Each one curated by different hands.

🔥 The fire

Nightly tending. s’mores. stories. the “how did we end up talking about this?” conversations.

🎣 The dock

A place to sit and stare at an illusion—cracking sounds included.

🚗 The car

Bench seats. radio. secret transmissions. a tiny time capsule you can climb into.

🧰 Built for after

Designed with reuse in mind—quality build, second life after debut.
