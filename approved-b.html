Meet Parker

She was never supposed
to be ordinary.

Parker Stone is almost sixty and looks thirty. She has red hair that catches fire in sunlight, eyes that shift color like the sea, and a fierce, defiant streak she never outgrew.

She's spent decades not fitting in, building walls, running every morning before the Florida heat gets bad. Feeling, always, like she's missed something she was supposed to do.

Then came the horse. And nothing about her life made sense in the same way again.

Defiant

Labeled trouble her whole life for refusing to go along. She never outgrew it. That's not a flaw. That's the point.

Gifted

Animals seek her out. She reads languages she never studied. A lifetime of chalking it up to instinct.

Called

Something ancient is woven into her bloodline. She didn't know. Until now.
