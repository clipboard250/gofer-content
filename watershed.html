03 / about

A designer trained as an ecologist.

Joe Howard is a California-licensed landscape architect and restoration ecologist whose twenty-year practice spans the Pacific West. Previously a principal at Harvey & Associates, he opened his own studio in 2026 to work more closely with land trusts, tribal governments, and landowners on long-horizon restoration projects.

His work has been cited in peer-reviewed journals, featured in agency guidance on tidal-marsh restoration, and recognized by the California chapter of the ASLA. He teaches graduate seminars on restoration practice, sits on the board of a Northern California land trust, and still spends Saturdays keying out plants in the field.
