Living Spaces Gardening livingspacesgardening.com

Who They Are

  • Tampa Bay / St. Pete homeowner, ages 35-65
  • Owns their home, likely 3+ years in Florida
  • Household income $75k+, pays for quality when they trust you
  • Environmentally conscious, cares about birds, butterflies, pollinators
  • Frustrated with high water bills or a lawn that never looks right
  • Saw a stunning native yard and thought: I want that

What They Want

  • A beautiful yard that doesn't require constant work
  • To feel good about what they're doing for the environment
  • Someone who knows Florida, not a generic landscaper
  • Low-maintenance, drought-tolerant plants that actually thrive here
  • Trust: credentials, photos of real work, a real person

How They Find You

  • Instagram or TikTok (visual, before/after content)
  • Neighbor referral or word of mouth
  • Google: "Florida native landscaping St. Petersburg"
  • Homes & Gardens feature or editorial mentions

What They're Afraid Of

  • Spending money on something that dies or looks bad
  • A contractor who doesn't understand native plants
  • HOA pushback on non-traditional landscaping
Copy direction: warm, reassuring, visual. Lead with beauty. Follow with ecology.

Living Restorations livingrestorations.com

Who They Are

  • Coastal or waterfront property owner with a restoration need
  • HOA or property manager with a pond, wetland, or shoreline
  • Developer, municipality, or land trust seeking permitted work
  • Environmental consultant looking for a credentialed partner
  • Sophisticated buyer: reads the scope, asks about methodology

What They Want

  • Science-based work from someone with real credentials
  • Compliance: permits, documentation, proper species selection
  • A contractor who understands coastal ecosystems, not just plants
  • Long-term ecological outcomes, not just aesthetics
  • Proof of credibility: FANN, Master Naturalist, project results

How They Find You

  • Professional referral: engineer, architect, county planner
  • Google: "coastal restoration St. Petersburg FL"
  • LinkedIn or industry directories
  • Direct outreach to you as a known FANN professional

What They're Afraid Of

  • Hiring someone who can't navigate permitting
  • Ecological missteps that create liability or re-work
  • A vendor who looks good online but lacks field experience
Copy direction: precise, science-forward, credentialed. Lead with expertise. Follow with outcomes.
