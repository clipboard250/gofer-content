Joe Howard is an artist and licensed landscape architect in California (CA License #4598). With more than 30 years of experience leading multidisciplinary design teams—engineers, architects, lighting professionals, and public works departments—Joe specializes in translating complex site conditions into built environments that belong to their place. He has integrated large-scale art and structures into parks, plazas, and public rights of way, working closely with design review boards, planning commissions, and regulatory agencies.
