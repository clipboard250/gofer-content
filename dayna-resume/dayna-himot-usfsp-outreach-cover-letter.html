Dear Members of the Search Committee,

I am writing to submit my application for the Associate Director of Outreach position at the University of South Florida St. Petersburg campus. I am a five year resident of St. Petersburg with more than two decades of experience in outreach, partnership-building, volunteer and program coordination, and public-facing roles across higher education, community initiatives, media, and service-oriented organizations. I am strongly drawn to USF’s commitment to community engagement and public service, and I welcome the opportunity to support the campus in strengthening its ties within St. Petersburg, Pinellas County and South Florida.

My background includes university-based work, multi-stakeholder program leadership, and coordination of large, complex projects with diverse partners. At Metropolitan State University of Denver, I served as Managing Editor and Teaching Assistant in the Social Documentary Journalism program, helping guide the community storytelling project Small Towns Colorado. In that capacity, I worked with faculty, students, and local organizations to support reporting and publishing that reflected community realities and priorities. In parallel, I served for seven years as a coordinator and later a seasonal steward for the Burning Man Project, where I supported year-round outreach, scheduling, training, and on-site logistics for hundreds of volunteers, artists, and partner teams.

Those roles required careful listening, consistent communication, and the ability to translate broad goals into practical plans, events, and communication systems. In my current practice as the founder of Gofer Content, based here in St. Petersburg, I work with small businesses, service organizations, and local initiatives to clarify their messaging, extend their outreach, and share information with neighbors in direct, accessible language. I am comfortable operating at the intersection of institutional priorities and community needs, and I understand the importance of reciprocity when building and sustaining partnerships.

The Associate Director of Outreach position calls for someone who can serve as a visible representative of USF St. Petersburg, coordinate campus-based outreach efforts, strengthen relationships with civic leaders and community organizations, and support faculty, staff, and students in connecting teaching, research, and service with community-identified needs. These responsibilities align closely with my experience. I have supervised and supported volunteers and student contributors; helped plan and deliver workshops and public programs; represented organizations at community meetings and events; and maintained communication across departments, partners, and participants in complex, fast-moving environments.

I am confident that my background in outreach, coordination, and partnership development, combined with my deep connection to St. Petersburg, would allow me to contribute meaningfully to USF’s Office of University Community Partnerships and to the St. Petersburg campus. I would be honored to help advance the university’s goals around community-engaged learning, research, and service.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I welcome the opportunity to speak with you about how my experience and perspective can support this role and the broader mission of USF St. Petersburg.

Sincerely,
Dayna Himot
