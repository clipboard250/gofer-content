Cover Letter — Associate Director of Outreach, USF St. Petersburg

Dayna Himot • St. Petersburg, FL • 727-353-9673 • dayna@gofercontent.com

Dear Search Committee,

I am writing to express my interest in the Associate Director of Outreach position at the University of South Florida St. Petersburg campus. I am a long-time St. Petersburg resident with more than two decades of experience in outreach, partnership development, volunteer coordination, program leadership, and public-facing roles across higher education, community initiatives, media, events, and service-oriented organizations. I am aligned with USF’s commitment to community-engaged learning and public service, and I would welcome the opportunity to help strengthen the campus’s connection to the city I call home.

My background blends university-based work, multi-stakeholder program coordination, and community-driven operations. At Metropolitan State University of Denver, I supported faculty and students as Managing Editor and Teaching Assistant in the journalism program, helping coordinate the community storytelling project Small Towns Colorado with local organizations across the region. Alongside this, I served for seven years as a coordinator and later a seasonal steward for the Burning Man Project, guiding year-round outreach, scheduling, volunteer support, and cross-department logistics for hundreds of participants, artists, community groups, and internal teams.

These roles taught me how to build trust, listen first, and translate community priorities into practical programs, events, and communication frameworks. In my current work as founder of Gofer Content here in St. Petersburg, I support small businesses, service groups, and local organizations with accessible messaging, outreach, and simple digital infrastructure. I routinely collaborate with community partners, neighborhood groups, and small organizations that serve families, students, and residents across the city.

The Associate Director of Outreach role calls for someone who can be a visible representative for USFSP, coordinate campus-based outreach efforts, strengthen relationships across civic and nonprofit sectors, and support faculty, staff, and students in connecting teaching, research, and service to community-identified needs. These are responsibilities I know well. I have supervised volunteers and student contributors, coordinated workshops and multi-day public activities, represented organizations at community meetings and events, and supported cross-campus and cross-department communication throughout my career.

Thank you for considering my application. I would welcome the chance to discuss how my background, lived experience, and community-rooted approach can support the goals of the Office of University Community Partnerships and the USF St. Petersburg campus.

Sincerely,
Dayna Himot
