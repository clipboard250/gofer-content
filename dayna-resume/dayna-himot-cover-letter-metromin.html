Dear Ms. Thatcher,

I am applying for the Seasonal Volunteer Coordinator role because your mission reflects how I try to live and work. From 2016 to 2023 I served year-round as a Coordinator of Volunteers with the Burning Man Project, organizing crews, schedules, and documentation, and creating on-site signage and safety reminders. I currently serve seasonally as a DPW Man Pavilion Shop Steward, overseeing tools, logistics, and volunteer safety for a 25-person crew. Alongside that work, I founded GOFER CONTENT, a small communications studio that gives me the flexibility to serve during the holidays.

My volunteer coordination experience began even earlier, managing teams for the Boulder International Fringe Festival and the Gaia Film Festival. I have also mentored youth journalists for the Denver Post’s Youth J-Day. I am comfortable with Salesforce and Google Workspace, and I care deeply about presenting a welcoming, organized environment for volunteers and guests. I live in St. Petersburg and see the daily need in our community, which is why I want to help your team serve families with dignity this season.

Thank you for considering my application. I would be honored to support Metropolitan Ministries’ holiday efforts through dependable coordination, clear communication, and a servant-minded approach.
