Re:  Request for Qualifications — Art Installation for the Coldstream Rd / Donner Pass Rd / I-80 Roundabout
Dear Members of the Public Art Commission of Truckee,

Misfit Toys Art is pleased to submit our qualifications for the Coldstream Road / Donner Pass Road / I-80 Roundabout public art commission. We have assembled a team specifically suited to this site: a licensed landscape architect with more than 30 years of experience leading multidisciplinary teams alongside public agencies, a fabrication lead who moves monumental steel from shop to site on deadline, and a founder whose permanent installations in San Francisco's Golden Gate Park have welcomed millions of visitors in collaboration with the San Francisco Recreation and Parks Department.

This roundabout marks that "are we there yet, mom/dad/aunt?" moment—the excitement of finally arriving in Truckee and the greater Lake Tahoe area after a long journey of counting mile markers. The public art piece that lands here should welcome everyone and make them feel at home.

Misfit Toys LLC's working model aligns with the Town's preferred approach: we fabricate offsite and engineer footings for Public Works installation. We are experienced collaborating with planning departments, design review boards, public works staff, and the diverse communities public art ultimately serves.

The enclosed statement of interest, team resume, and portfolio detail our qualifications. We would be honored to advance to the concept design phase and look forward to the Commission's review.
