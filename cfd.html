Quick answers

Is this political or tied to one faith?

No. It’s neighbor-to-neighbor help—welcoming everyone and respecting every background. We keep the tone simple, kind, and practical.

What about confidentiality?

We only use your details to coordinate pickups or volunteering. We don’t publish personal info or add you to lists.

Can I give money?

At this time we focus on in-kind donations and local partnerships. If you’d like to help with costs (boxes, gas cards), note that in the volunteer form.

What shouldn’t I donate?

Opened or expired food, glass jars without padding, and anything leaking. For baby items, sealed only.
