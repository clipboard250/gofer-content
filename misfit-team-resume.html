MARK DEEM

Founder & Lead Artist

mark@misfittoys.art
650-302-3018

Biomedical engineer by training and former expert witness before the U.S. House of Representatives on FDA and patent law, Mark brings technical rigor to large-scale public art. He is known for keeping budgets on target, timelines on track, and structures built for durability and long-term public use. His installations have welcomed more than 8 million visitors to Golden Gate Park’s Golden Mile corridor.

Large-scale steel design · Welding and metal fabrication · Project management · Budget planning and cost estimation · Public installation and site logistics · Concept development · Team leadership and crew management · Technical documentation · Public agency compliance · CAD and technical drawing
