What We Studied

What Shami Stovall
Is Doing Right

You saw her X ad. 976,000 impressions. Her organic posts hit 12K views. Here's what she does that almost no one else does consistently — and how we apply every piece of it to Parker.

The Hook Formula
Every post leads with a comparison hook — "Pokémon meets Harry Potter," "John Wick meets High Fantasy," "Groundhog Day meets urban fantasy." Same hooks rotated across months.
Cover Image Every Time
The book cover appears in every single post. Organic or paid. The visual trains the algorithm and the audience at the same time.
One Engagement Question
She ends with a reader question: "Which creature would YOU bond with?" It generates replies. Replies feed the algorithm. Simple, repeatable, effective.
Repetition Is the Strategy
She doesn't try to be clever every time. She repeats the same angles with the same cover until the audience recognizes it before they read a word.
Paid Ads + Organic = Same Post
Her paid X ads use the exact same format as her organic posts. No special "ad creative." What performs organically gets budget behind it.
Author Personality Stays In
Her bio says she loves John, video games, and talking about herself in the 3rd person. The personality isn't separate from the brand — it is the brand.

This is Shami's ad platform and it should be Parker's too. Organic first, paid behind what performs. The audience here is adult fantasy readers, Celtic mythology enthusiasts, and women 35–65 who read literary fiction.

Paid Ad Plan

Take whichever Hook Post performs best organically (most replies, most impressions) and put $5–$10/day behind it. Target: women 35–65, interests in Celtic mythology, historical fiction, fantasy. Same post, same image. No separate ad creative needed. This is exactly Shami's method.

Story Polls

2–3× / week
Quick engagement to feed the algorithm "Parker's hair — fiery red or deep auburn?" / "Which would you choose: immortality or Celtic magic?" / "Enbarr arrived through a storm cloud. Would you run or stay?"

Cover Reveal Event

One-time milestone
Plan this as a moment, not a post Story countdown stickers for 3 days before. Reel for the reveal itself. Static post with the cover lives on the grid permanently. This is the first big organic surge.

Hashtag Strategy

Use 8–12 per post. Mix: large (#FantasyBooks, #BookTok, #BookstagramFantasy) + niche (#CelticMythology, #CelticFantasy, #WomensFantasy, #NewFantasyBooks2026, #ParkerStone). Niche hashtags drive the right readers. Large tags drive discovery.

Facebook Community Building

Facebook is where Parker Stone's core reader demographic lives — women 40–65 who read fantasy, mythology, and women's fiction. Longer posts work here. Community works here.

Full Character Posts

2× / week
Share the full "Meet Parker" description Facebook readers will actually read it. Post the character description, the Enbarr mythology context, the "why this story matters." Cover image every time.
"Parker Stone is almost sixty and looks thirty. She has red hair that catches fire in sunlight, eyes that shift color like the sea, and a fierce, defiant streak she never outgrew. She spent decades not fitting in. Then came the horse. [Read more → parkerstone.com]"

Book Group Outreach

Ongoing
Target specific Facebook groups — join and post as Pam Celtic Mythology Lovers · Women's Fantasy Readers · BookTok Recommendations · Irish Mythology & Folklore. Post the hook, the cover, the waitlist link. Follow group rules.

Mythology Education

1× / week
One Celtic mythology fact tied back to the book "Did you know Enbarr was so fast he could cross the ocean in one stride? He appears in my book — here's what the mythology actually says..." This positions Pam as an authority, not just an author.

Waitlist Pushes

Every 2 weeks
Direct call to action — join the list at parkerstone.com Facebook allows links. Use them. Don't bury the CTA. "Book comes out 2026. Be first to know → [link]." Keep it simple.

The Roadmap

Pre-Launch
Timeline

Now → May

Foundation Phase

Start posting 3× per week. Cover image every post. Rotate the 5 hook lines. Build the waitlist at parkerstone.com. Join 5 Facebook groups. Post the character description. The goal is recognition — people should start to see Parker's cover and know it.

May → Aug

Cover Reveal + Momentum

The cover reveal is your first big event. Build to it with Story countdowns on IG. Post it across all platforms same day. Start putting $5–10/day in X ads behind the Hook Post that performed best. Begin beta reader outreach and start collecting blurbs.

Aug → Launch

Pre-Order Push + Creator Outreach

Add the pre-order link to every post. Begin outreach to BookTok creators and Celtic mythology podcasts. Send the Creator Brief (hook library + synopsis + suggested angles). Let them make it their own. Increase ad budget on X to $20/day as launch approaches.

Launch Week

Publish Day + Launch Sprint

Post on all platforms every day launch week. Boost every post. Ask every beta reader and early follower to leave an Amazon review the day it drops. A sprint of reviews in the first 48 hours signals to Amazon's algorithm that the book has momentum.
