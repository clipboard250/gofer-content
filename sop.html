GOFER CONTENT SOP: Podcast Research & Outreach Strategy

Purpose: A repeatable system for identifying and pitching high-fit podcasts aligned with a client’s message, goals, and audience.

1) Define the objective

  • What’s being promoted? (book, service, story)
  • One big message / POV
  • Ideal audience + tone; any no-go topics

2) Targeting rubric (score 1–5 each)

Audience fit 40% · Credibility/format 25% · Topic relevance 20% · Referral value 10% · Production 5%. Prioritize total score ≥ 18.

3) Research sources

Apple Podcasts, Spotify, ListenNotes, Rephonic, YouTube (guest clips), IG/LinkedIn (host outreach), competitor guest maps.

4) Tracking columns

Podcast | Host | About the Host | Topics | Apple | Spotify | Ratings | Socials | Contact | Notes | Status

5) Outreach (150 words, friendly, no hype)

Subject: Guest idea for [Show Name]. Reference an episode, connect the guest to their audience, state topic, ask “Open to learning more?”

6) Follow-up

Follow-up in 7–10 days; max 2 touches per show; track results.

7) Post-booking

Confirm date/platform; send bio + talking points; archive live links; thank host.

8) Core principles

  1. Alignment beats reach.
  2. Clarity wins.
  3. Relationships compound.
  4. Pitch less, research more.
  5. Documentation = scale.

AI research prompt

“Create a ranked list of 15 podcasts for a [type of expert/client] about [themes]. Return: Podcast, Host, Topics, Apple, Spotify, Ratings, Socials, Contact, Notes.”
