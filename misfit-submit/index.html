About One Tin Soldier

Monumental children’s toys, weathered as if uncovered from another time — inviting us back into wonder, memory, and play. Built by a crew of misfits who believe art is community.

Get Involved — Volunteer

Meet the Misfits. We’re a crew from many eras of Burning Man — Man Krew, the Pavilion, the Pier Group, the Temple, and makers from around Black Rock City. Professionals and first-timers. Artists and builders. Architects, electricians, welders, woodworkers. We’re here because this project speaks to us.

If that sounds like you, jump in. Tell us your skills (or what you want to learn), your availability, and how to reach you.
