Scope of services

Line edit and light copy edit for Parker Stone and the Legend of Enbarr

Client: Pam Gaylor
Editor: Dayna Himot, Gofer Content
Service type: Line edit with light copy edit
Flat rate: Six hundred dollars (friend rate)
Estimated hours: Twenty to twenty five hours
Hourly equivalent: Twenty five dollars per hour

1. Purpose

This is a refinement edit. The writing is already strong. The goal is to smooth the prose, tighten pacing, and offer natural alternatives while preserving the author’s style. This is not a structural or developmental edit.

2. Scope of services

2.1 Line by line refinement

I recommend the following where it strengthens the chapter.

  • Smoothing long or dense sentences
  • Adjusting pacing inside scenes
  • Offering alternate phrasing that keeps the tone and improves flow
  • Removing unintentional repetition of ideas or imagery
  • Improving rhythm between sentences and paragraphs

2.2 Light copy edit

I recommend a light pass for:

  • Minor grammar and tense corrections
  • Consistency checks
  • Small adjustments where a sentence is unclear or overly complex

2.3 Repetition review

I recommend noting places where the same word or concept appears close together and suggesting options. For example, in the opening scene the word “reflection” appears more than once. Natural alternatives include:

  • “the face staring back at her”
  • “the woman in the mirror”

2.4 Tone and word choice

I recommend suggesting alternatives for modern terms that can feel mechanical in a Celtic and mythic setting, while keeping your intent.

  • Chaos alternatives: turmoil, upheaval, confusion, inner storm
  • Clarity alternatives: understanding, things coming into focus, the fog lifting, a settled mind

2.5 Margin notes

I will leave light comments in the file where helpful. These will be optional suggestions about pacing, character intention, or specific sentences that may benefit from a small adjustment.

3. Out of scope

The following are not included in the flat rate and can be discussed separately if desired.

  • Developmental editing
  • Structural changes or reorganization of chapters
  • Scene or chapter rewrites
  • Multiple revision cycles
  • Proofreading after author revisions
  • Formatting for print or digital distribution

4. Deliverables

  • Edited manuscript file with tracked changes
  • Margin notes with optional suggestions
  • Brief follow up conversation if needed after delivery
Summary. This scope covers a refinement pass that smooths, tightens, and polishes the manuscript while keeping the author’s tone and style intact. All edits are suggestions and the author has final say on any changes.
