1. Purpose

This is a refinement edit. The manuscript already has a strong foundation. The goal is to smooth the prose, adjust pacing, refine phrasing, and reduce AI-like language while preserving the author’s tone.

2. Scope of services

2.1 Line by line refinement

  • Smoothing long or dense sentences
  • Adjusting pacing inside scenes
  • Offering alternative phrasing that keeps tone intact
    • <
  • Removing unintentional repetition
  • Improving rhythm between paragraphs

2.2 Light copy edit

  • Minor grammar and tense corrections
  • Consistency checks
  • Small adjustments for clarity

2.3 Repetition review

I will note repeated phrasing and suggest options, for example:

  • “the face staring back at her”
  • “the woman in the mirror”

2.4 Tone and removal of AI-like language

  • Chaos options: turmoil, upheaval, confusion, inner storm
  • Clarity options: understanding, things coming into focus, the fog lifting

2.5 Margin notes

Light comments for pacing, emotional beats, or sentence refinement.

3. Out of scope

  • Developmental editing
  • Structural changes
  • Scene rewrites
  • Multiple revision cycles
  • Proofreading after revisions
  • Formatting for print or digital editions

4. Deliverables

  • Edited manuscript with tracked changes
  • Margin notes with optional suggestions
  • Brief follow up conversation if needed
