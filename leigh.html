The photographer

A photojournalist's eye. A Gulfport heart.

Leigh Clifton has been making photographs for 26 years. She earned a BA in Photojournalism and Communications, went back to school in 2010 for a Commercial Photography degree, and spent three years freelancing for the Washington Post alongside two Pulitzer Prize-winning photographers.

She covered high school sports for the Tampa Bay Times for two years. She is the current Editor of Gulfport Living Magazine, where she writes cover stories, shoots the cover photo, and lays out the entire publication every month. This is how she shoots portraits, too: with a journalist's eye for the real moment, not the posed version of it.

She lives in Gulfport, a funky, artistic beach town that is part of St. Petersburg. She shoots on location across Tampa Bay and knows every beautiful spot. She is particularly known for golden hour and sunset work, and does a lot of surprise engagements at the water's edge.

How it works

Simple from the first message.

01

Reach out

Text or email Leigh directly. She offers a complimentary phone consultation with every new client and responds quickly.

02

Pick a location

Leigh knows Tampa Bay. She can suggest the right spot based on the time of day, the look you want, and the season. She travels to you, or you can come to Gulfport.

03

The session

Leigh shoots the real moments. She uses natural light whenever possible and prefers natural surfaces over generic backdrops. You will not have to overthink it.

04

Digital delivery

After editing, Leigh sends a download link directly to you. No prints, no photo books, no waiting for a package. Just your files, ready to use.
