Phase 2: JoinBrands Activation (Optional)

What is JoinBrands?

JoinBrands is a creator marketplace. Here's how it works:

  1. You post ONE campaign using one of my scripts
  2. Multiple creators apply to your campaign
  3. You approve the creators you like (typically 5-12 creators)
  4. Each approved creator makes ONE video based on your script
  5. Creators submit their videos for your approval
  6. You approve the videos you want to use
  7. Creators either post to their feed OR send you raw files to use as ads

Result: 12 approved creators = 12 unique videos from different voices, all promoting Emersoft.

Real Example: Bloom (wellness brand) approved 700 creators in 10 days, got 700 unique videos, reached 20M+ people, and became the top Prime Day brand in their category.
