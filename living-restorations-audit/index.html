02

Website Audit

What's Broken and Why

!

Two Domains, One Business — SEO Authority Split

Running two websites for the same business splits all link equity, domain authority, and search signals. Google doesn't know which is the "real" site. Neither ranks. You're competing against yourself.

!

robots.txt Blocking All Search Engines — Both Sites

Both domains have robots.txt files telling Google, Bing, and every search engine to stay away. Neither site appears in search results. Zero organic traffic is possible with this configuration.

!

"Under Construction" Pages Live on livingrestorations.com

Service pages are published with "THIS PAGE IS UNDER CONSTRUCTION" banners visible to anyone who visits. This destroys credibility with potential clients.

⚠

Location-Stuffing Footer on livingspacesgardening.com

The footer contains a raw list of every Florida city. This is a known Google penalty trigger — it signals spam, not legitimate service coverage.

⚠

Generic AI-Generated Copy Throughout

Both sites contain template-style content that reads like AI-generated first drafts. Phrases like "embracing values of trust, innovation, and Earth stewardship" are filler. Kiamesha's real voice and expertise are absent from these pages.

⚠

No Schema Markup or Structured Data

Neither site has LocalBusiness schema or structured data. Google can't display rich results (star ratings, service areas, hours) in search.

⚠

No OG Tags or Social Preview Cards

Sharing either URL on Facebook, iMessage, or LinkedIn shows a generic preview with no image, no description, no brand presence. Every share is a missed opportunity.

i

Rebolt Platform Limitations

Rebolt is an AI-powered website builder for home service businesses. While convenient, it produces cookie-cutter templates with limited SEO control and defaults that actively harm search performance. Moving off Rebolt is the right call.

!

Google Cannot See Either Website

A robots.txt with Disallow: / on both domains means zero pages are indexed. Running site:livingrestorations.com confirms no pages appear in Google. You are invisible to anyone searching for ecological restoration in Florida.

No Sitemap Submitted

Neither site has a sitemap.xml. Even once robots.txt is fixed, Google won't know which pages to prioritize.

⚠

Duplicate Content — Same Reviews on All Location Pages

livingspacesgardening.com has dozens of location pages (Pinellas, Hillsborough, Pasco, Manatee, Tampa, St. Pete, etc.) with identical testimonials and near-identical copy — just the city name swapped. Google treats this as thin, duplicate content.

⚠

E-E-A-T Signals Wasted

Kiamesha has exceptional credentials not being leveraged: Florida Master Naturalist certification, first FANN credentialed professionals in Florida, Homes & Gardens magazine feature (Nov 2025), 7+ years of project work, named client testimonials. None of this is structured for Google to understand or reward.

05

Untapped Opportunities

What's Being Left on the Table

Authority

"As Featured in Homes & Gardens" Credibility Bar

A press mention from a major publication is a trust signal most competitors will never have. An "As Seen In" bar on the website, social bios, and GBP would immediately elevate perceived authority.

SEO

Zero Competition for "Ecological Restoration + [Florida City]"

Most competitors target "landscaping" or "lawn care." The ecological restoration / living shoreline / dune restoration niche has relatively low keyword competition but high intent. Rankings could come quickly with a properly indexed site.

Content

Before/After Project Case Studies

Kiamesha's work is visual and dramatic. Before/after galleries with project narratives serve triple duty: website SEO content, social media content, and prospect trust-building.

Video

YouTube Channel for Project Walkthroughs

Video content showing restoration process, plant selection, and completed projects would rank in YouTube search and embed into the website. Educational content about living shorelines and dune restoration has strong search volume.

Local SEO

Google Business Profile Optimization

A fully optimized GBP with proper categories, service area, photos, reviews, and posts would significantly improve Google Maps and local pack visibility.

Brand

Domain Consolidation = Immediate Authority Boost

Consolidating two weak domains into one strong one with 301 redirects combines link equity and gives Google a single, clear signal.
